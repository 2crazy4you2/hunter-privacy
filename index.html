🦅 Hunter — Privacy Policy

Application: Hunter (Discord App ID 1493193581303693433)
Last updated: 12 June 2026

Hunter ("the bot", "we", "us") is an automated anti-scam and moderation bot for Discord servers. It detects and removes phishing links, crypto-scam screenshots, and invite spam. This policy explains what data Hunter processes, why, how long it is kept, who it is shared with, and how to request its deletion. By adding Hunter to a server, or by sending messages in a server where Hunter is present, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. What data we process

Hunter only processes data needed to detect and act on scams and to maintain moderation state.

DataPurposeRetention
Message content (text, embeds, attachments) of messages posted in servers where Hunter is active Scanned in real time for phishing links, scam wording, and malicious images (perceptual hashing, OCR, URL resolution, AI classification) Processed transiently in memory and discarded. Not retained as a general archive.
Short message previews (up to ~1,000 characters) Attached to an active moderation record (moderator review card or community-vote record) so moderators can see what was actioned Retained with the moderation record

Account metadata (account age, server-join date, roles, avatar presence) Account-risk scoring. Read from the message event on demand; the full member list is not streamed or cached. Not stored separately; evaluated at scan time
Hunter does not collect Discord passwords or authentication tokens, does not read presence/activity status, and does not sell or rent any data to anyone.

2. Legal basis & scope

Hunter processes message content under Discord's Message Content privileged intent strictly for the purpose of automated scam and spam moderation. Scanning is server-wide because, like Discord's own AutoMod, scam detection cannot function if individual users could exempt themselves. There is therefore no per-user opt-out of scanning; users who do not wish to be scanned should not post in servers where Hunter is active.

3. Where data is stored & security

Persisted data (reputation, moderation records, message previews, threat database) is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted outside of Discord. Supabase encrypts data at rest (AES-256) on the underlying storage. All outbound URL fetches performed by the bot are validated against private/reserved IP ranges to prevent server-side request forgery (SSRF).

Message content sent to Groq and Google is used for real-time inference only. Hunter does not use any message content to train machine-learning or AI models.

5. Data retention

Message content is processed transiently and discarded. Message previews are retained only as long as their associated moderation record exists. Reputation and moderation-enforcement data is retained for as long as it remains relevant to moderation (strike counts decay after 30 days of inactivity, though the underlying record persists). You may request earlier deletion at any time (Section 6).

6. Your rights — access & deletion

You may request access to, or deletion of, data Hunter holds about you. To do so:

  • Email crazneaxbusiness@gmail.com, including your Discord user ID; or
  • Ask a moderator of the relevant server, who can reset or remove your moderation record using the bot's mod commands.

We will action verified deletion requests within a reasonable period.

7. Children

Hunter is intended for use on Discord, which requires users to meet Discord's minimum age requirement. Hunter is not directed at children below that age.
